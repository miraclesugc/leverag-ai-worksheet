Custom Instructions — Type These Into Claude During Step 8

Use this as a reference while typing your instructions directly into Claude. Be specific — the more specific, the better your results.

I AM A
your role — e.g. marketing consultant, founder
Job title or how you'd describe yourself professionally
I USE THIS FOR
what this specific project is for
Be specific — not just "work" but which area or client
I NEED HELP WITH
writing / planning / research / content / other
ALWAYS
format preference — short paragraphs, bullets, etc.
NEVER
jargon / long intros / emojis / fluff
OTHER CONTEXT CLAUDE NEEDS TO KNOW

Examples for reference:

Example 1 — Job Seeker

I am a UX designer with 5 years of experience in fintech.

I use this for my job search — applying to senior UX roles.

I need help with cover letters, tailoring resume bullets, interview prep.

Always write confidently and directly. Keep cover letters under 300 words.

Never use "I am passionate about" or generic filler phrases.

Context Target: mid-size tech startups, remote/hybrid, $120k–$150k range.

Example 2 — Small Business Owner

I am the founder of a boutique event planning company in Philadelphia.

I use this for marketing and content — Instagram captions, emails, promo copy.

I need help with writing content that attracts and retains clients.

Always warm, energetic, conversational tone. Short sentences.

Never corporate, stiff, or generic. No buzzwords.

Context Audience: women 28–45 planning weddings and milestone events.

03

To Explore Presets
04

05

06
